## Full body text description
*(min 50 words)*

DBV Teleprompter is a native, professional teleprompter application for video creators, presenters, educators, and streamers: fast, lightweight, and 100% private — no accounts, no ads, and no telemetry.

Engineered with a sleek "Studio Instrument" matte-black aesthetic optimized for recording studios and low-light environments. Easily import script files (.txt, .md), save and export changes using native operating system dialogs, drag and drop files directly onto the editor, and fine-tune scrolling speed and typography size with pixel precision.

Calculates real-time word count and estimated speech duration in minutes and seconds, dynamically adjusting to your chosen reading pace.

**Key Features:**
• Professional "Studio Instrument" UI with high-contrast Dark and Light themes
• Native file management: Open and Save scripts (.txt, .md) with OS file dialogs
• Full Drag & Drop support to drop script files straight into the editor
• Dynamic word count and real-time estimated speaking duration
• Universal desktop shortcuts (⌘S/Ctrl+S save, ⌘O/Ctrl+O open, ⌘Enter/Ctrl+Enter start, Escape exit)
• Full playback control during recording: Space to play/pause, +/- for speed, PageUp/PageDown for font size, and arrow keys for fine nudging
• Slide-in panel to customize and persist all keyboard shortcuts to your preferences
• Reading focus guide with contrast indicator to keep your eye line locked on camera
• High-legibility, self-hosted brand fonts (Newsreader and Nunito)
• Native macOS application menu (App/Edit/Window) and instant startup (<200 ms)
• Fully bilingual interface (English and Spanish) with instant switching
• 100% private and secure: your scripts never leave your device, works completely offline

Open source under the MIT License developed by David Bueno Vallejo.
